Strengths of the ELL Series

Industry-Leading SI Performance

  • Ideal for next-generation, high-speed, and high-frequency applications including 800G/1.6T systems.
  • Proven top-of-class SI performance compared to competitor materials even with lower grade glass fabric.

Flexible Glass Cloth Selection

  • Multiple reinforcement grades are available for different SI requirements: Low Dk1 (NE), Low Dk2 (NER), and Q glass.
  • Excellent SI performance even with Low Dk1 glass, enabling stable supply, good processability, and higher manufacturing yield.

Consistent product design based on
AGC's proprietary materials technology

  • Suitable for hybrid design with compatible resin chemistry, which ensures good processability and material homogeneity.

Excellent Low-CTE Performance

  • Enhances PCB thermal reliability and helps reduce warpage during PCB fabrication and assembly.
