    Precautions In Handling

    • Take care in handling thin core laminates as they are easily damaged
    • The grain direction is indicated on the label with an arrow
    • Laminates exceeding 24 months can still be used as long as the copper foil has no severe oxidation or stain

    Inner Layer Process

    • Tooling & pinning holes should be drilled or routed. Punching is forbidden

    • Lay the panel on a flat clean surface. The solvent clean should be followed with a clean water rinse

    • Remove copper oxides using commercially available cleaners and micro etchants

    • Abrasive cleaning (e.g., pumice powder or conveyorized brush scrubbing) is not recommended
    • Mechanical surface preparation tends to cause microscopic scratches in copper. These scratches become stress risers that can lead to thermal stress cracks in some environmental conditions. While strongly recommending against abrasive cleaning, we recognize that in some circumstances that abrasive cleaning is necessary. When abrasive cleaning is required, it should be performed with minimal pressure and knowledge of the risks involved. Brush scrubbing may cause material distortion
    • After oxide, it is preferred to press cores within 12 hours. Any cores staged more than 24 hours should be re-treated

    Desmearing Process

    Plasma treatment is a necessary process, and materials should be baked more than 1 hour at 120℃ before plasma treatment. Meanwhile, material cannot be subjected to high-pressure washing and scrubbing before metallisation. Plasma: Half time of FR4 conditions is recommended.

    Pressure (mT) Temperature (℃) Gas Mixture Power (W) Duration (min)
    250 90 90% H2, 10% N2 4500 5
    250 100 10% CF, 90% N2 4000 10
    250 100 100% O2 4000 5
    250 100 100% N2 4200 30

    Permanganate Desmear: Not recommended.

    Plating and Solder mask

    • VT-6702 laminates have been engineered to be highly resistant to chemical infiltration and a robust hole wall preparation process isnecessary for a successful deposition plating process. Following hole wall preparation, VT-6702 will accept standard electroless copper or direct deposit metallization plating process.
    • When copper surface preparation is required, chemical cleaning processes are preferred.Mechanical scrubbing should be avoided due to possible mechanical damage. Otherwise, standard processing should be used.
    • No special treatment is required if the surface has not been mechanically scrubbed. In rare cases, where adhesion is poor, a plasma treatment may be used to activate the exposed PTFE.

    • Rigid phenolic entry and a rigid backer should be used. In some cases, adding paper (white paper or Kraft paper) between the phenolic and the part allows better conformance to surface topography and may reduce burring

    • Materials will generally provide a better edge quality due to none glass fabric

    Packing and Baking Recommendations

    • It is recommended to bake the board before packaging at 125°C/4~8h to avoid moisture causing a decrease in heat resistance
    • If the PCBs needs to be stored for a long time before use, it is recommended to use aluminum foil vacuum packaging
    • If exceed 3 months after packaging, It is best to bake the PCBs at 125°C/4~6h before assembly before use
