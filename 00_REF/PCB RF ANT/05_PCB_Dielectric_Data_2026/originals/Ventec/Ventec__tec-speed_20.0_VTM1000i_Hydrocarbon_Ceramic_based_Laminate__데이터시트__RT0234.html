  • tec-speed 20.0 VTM1000i Laminate

    UL Approval: E214381 Version: 20/11/2024 /280

    Precautions in Handling

    Storage Condition & Shelf Life

    Laminate
    Storage Condition Temperature Room
    Relative Humidity /
    Shelf Life 24 Months (airproof)

    Designing and Inner Layer Process

    • Tooling & pinning holes should be drilled or Punching is forbidden
    • Dimension stability is superior to Standard FR4
    • Please consult with your oxide vendor to make sure that our material is fully compatible with your oxide process. We recommend controlling the peel strength to over 2 lb.fin
    • After oxide, it is preferred to press cores within 12 hours. Any cores staged more than 24 hours should be re-treated
    • Please confirm the grinding treatment process as the material was soft and brittle. Please don't use the grinding process for the inner and outer layer with large area or full no copper design
