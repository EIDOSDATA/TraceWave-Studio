Technical Capability

The information below details some of the key technical capabilities that we are currently able to offer and support. It includes details related to materials, PCB technologies, and product types we are producing today, as well as examples of the tolerances we can achieve.

We categorize these capabilities into two levels: The first is what we refer to as standard, meaning we can supply each individual parameter from multiple approved sources. The second is our advanced capability, representing the highest level we can offer. However, in this category, supply chain options are more limited, and in some cases, only one of our approved partner factories may be capable of meeting the specific requirement.

This capability overview reflects our qualified production capacity as of January 1st, 2026, including all planned and approved investments for the first half of 2026.
More advanced technologies are also available at a lower qualification level, depending on agreement between NCAB, the factory, and the customer – for example, Ultra HDI and IC Substrate technologies, which are assessed on a case-by-case basis.

If you have questions regarding this, you can always consult your local NCAB technical contact person.

Materials

We are proud to say we only use globally recognized materials. However, due to new technologies in PCB production and the requirements of our customers’ application development, we see the need to qualify more sophisticated materials and new material development.

For this, we have a documented qualification procedure and can offer a range of qualified materials. All materials are additionally approved and qualified by our PSL (Preferred Supplier List) rated factories.
