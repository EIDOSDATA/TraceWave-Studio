DE156 is a base material of type FR-4. The resin matrix is based
on a modified epoxy resin; conventional E-glass-fabric is used for
                                                                                                        Dk 4.00, Df 0.02
reinforcement. The requirements of flammability class V-0 as per                                              /94
UL 94 are met without addition of antimony compounds. Since
this grade does not contain halogens, it displays greater thermal
stability than a standard FR-4, as time to delamination (T260)
measurements prove.
