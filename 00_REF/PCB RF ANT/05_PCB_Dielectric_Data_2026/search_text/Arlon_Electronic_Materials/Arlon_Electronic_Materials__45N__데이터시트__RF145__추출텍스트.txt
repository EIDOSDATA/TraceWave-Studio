 45N
Multifunctional Epoxy Laminate & Prepreg
                                  Arlon's 45N is a tough, high Tg (175°C by DSC)
                                  multifunctional epoxy laminate and prepreg system
                                  designed for use in a variety of higher layer count
                                  MLB’s. Processeable using conventional FR-4
                                  conditions




 Features:
             • Meets IPC4101/26 description and specification

             • High Tg by DSC (175°C)

             • Resists barrel cracking and inner layer copper cracking during fabrication

             • Resistant to Measling and Solder shock Defects

             • UL-94 VO

             • RoHS/WEEE Compliant

             • Suitable for most Lead-Free applications




 Typical Applications:

             • Automotive Under-hood
               applications

             • Backplanes and Mother Boards

             • Ball Grid Array Packaging

             • High layer count MLB’s
