                                                                                 Several approaches can be used
                                                                                 to reduce glass compression:
                                                                                 1. The simplest “fix” is simply to
                                                                                     reduce laminating pressure to
                                                                                     eliminate the excessive flow. In
                                                                                     this case care needs to be taken
                                                                                     that enough flow remains to fill
                                                                                     inner layer circuitry and to prevent
                                                                                     dryness in low pressure areas.
                                                                                 2. Slowing heating rate in
                                                                                     combination with reduced
                                                                                     lamination pressure to retain more
                                                                                     of the resin in the package. (Note:
                                                                                     This will also reduce the tendency
                                                                                     of a PWB to “football,” or to be
                                                                                     thinner at the edges than in the
                                                                                     center.)
