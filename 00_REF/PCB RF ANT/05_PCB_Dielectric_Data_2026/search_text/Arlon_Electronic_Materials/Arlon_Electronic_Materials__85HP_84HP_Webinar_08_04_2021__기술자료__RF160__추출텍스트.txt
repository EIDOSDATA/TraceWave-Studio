                  85HP High Performance Filled Polyimide Resin System
85HP a pure polyimide resin which incorporates a proprietary filler system, benefits are:

   ➢ Lower Z axis expansion (<1% between 50-260⁰)

   ➢ Improved hole wall drilling, reducing tear out and copper wicking in glass bundles

   ➢ Improved dimensional stability with balanced weave glass and proprietary filler

   ➢ 2.5X higher thermal conductivity than other polyimide systems for dissipating heat

   ➢ Reduces risk of resin cracking in neat resin areas during thermal excursions and
     mechanical drilling

   ➢ Allows for > 5X sequential lamination cycles and higher layer count MLB’s

   ➢ Bromine and Halogen free resin system

                           IPC-4101E-WAM1 Slash Sheets


➢ Slash sheets define and identify the constituency and performance requirements
  of laminates and prepregs.

➢ IPC-4101 slash sheets are important to allow the OEM or designer to compare
  material properties to the end application.

➢ 85HP and 84HP for example are slashed to 40, 41 and 43, each slash sheet has its
  own material properties.

➢ In the case of 85HP slashed to 43, the designer requires a 250⁰C Tg and a low Z-
  axis CTE of 1% or less between 50⁰C-260⁰C.

                                         85HP Overview


➢ The use of heavy copper and higher layer counts has spiked significantly over the last few years for
  high power applications.

➢ Processing these boards pose some challenges such as drilling, plating, thermal expansion, resin voids,
  resin cracking, board warping.

Arlon’s solution was the creation of 85HP and 84HP
➢ The benefits of 85HP are improved via and through hole drilling.
➢ Reduction in copper wicking.
➢ Consistent etch back.
➢ Reduced the propensity of resin cracking.
➢ Reduced resin shrinking.
➢ Low Z-axis expansion of less than 1%.
➢ Recently released 84HP uses the same 85N resin with a micro-fine proprietary filler coated on 50” 106
   glass and matches the grain direction of the 85HP.

                                Moisture Absorption of Polyimide MLB’s


➢ Polyimide resins tend to absorb moisture faster than other resin systems

➢ Removing moisture before processing is critical in high humidity conditions

➢ It has been determined that as little as 0.22% moisture can cause blistering or delamination in PI resins

➢ Both moisture pickup and subsequent drying are impacted by several variables such as PWB thickness,
  construction and finished coatings

➢ A polyimide board may get shipped without issues, but the assembly operation without precautions may
  run into blistering issues, the chart below illustrates how long boards can sit before they reach 0.22%

➢ Lowest Z-axis expanding polyimide resin system on the market, (<1% between 50⁰C-260⁰C).
➢ Meets IPC-4101E slash 40, 41 and 43.

➢ High resistance of wicking and resin cracking, even and controllable etchback during processing
➢ Capable of several sequential lamination cycles
➢ Consistent thickness control, uniform registration using balanced glass styles
➢ Predictable electrical stability across the plane due to balanced glass styles
