laminates do not require specialized via preparation processes such as sodium etch. This material is a rigid,
thermoset laminate that is capable of being processed by automated handling systems and scrubbing
equipment used for copper surface preparation.
RO4000 laminates are currently offered in various configurations utilizing both 1080 and 1674 glass fabric styles,
with all configurations meeting the same laminate electrical performance specification. Responding to the
need for higher Relative Thermal Index (RTI) values than 105°C, we have developed the RO4350B™ laminate,
which exhibits RTI values as high as 150°C. Specifically designed as a drop-in replacement for RO4350™
material, RO4350B laminate is the standard flame retardent product in the RO4000 product line. These
materials conform to the requirements of IPC-4103, slash sheet /10 for RO4003C and /11 for RO4350B.
