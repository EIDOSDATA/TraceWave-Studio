Low-Cost RF Substrate

 Benefits

      •    Low Df

 Applications

      •    LNBs

      •    PCS/PCN Large Format Antennas
      •    Passive Components




TLC-32 laminates are engineered to provide a cost-effective substrate suitable for a wide range of microwave applications. TLC-32
laminates offer superior electrical performance compared to thermoset laminates (e.g., FR-4, PPO, BT, polyimide, and cyanate ester).
TLC-32’s construction also provides exceptional mechanical stability. TLC-32 laminates can be sheared, drilled, milled, and plated
using standard methods for PTFE/woven fiberglass materials. The laminates are dimensionally stable and exhibit virtually no
moisture absorption during fabrication.
