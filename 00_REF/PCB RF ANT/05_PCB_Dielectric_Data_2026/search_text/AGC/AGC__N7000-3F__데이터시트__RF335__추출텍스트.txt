N7000-3F is a next-generation high-Tg polyimide using a toughened, filled resin chemistry. The product meets UL 94-V1
designation. N7000-3F is designed to prevent cracking when filling etched areas in polyimide multilayers that contain
heavy copper. This advanced material is designed for use in a wide variety of applications that include fine geometry
multilayer constructions and extreme reliability. This polyimide also meets NASA requirements for no visible bromine.
