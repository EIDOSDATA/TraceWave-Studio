Comparative Tracking Index (CTI)
Expressed as that voltage which causes tracking on a material after 50 drops of 0.1 percent ammonium chloride
solution has fallen. The results of testing the nominal 1/8 inch (3mm) thickness are considered representative of
the materials performance in any thickness.
