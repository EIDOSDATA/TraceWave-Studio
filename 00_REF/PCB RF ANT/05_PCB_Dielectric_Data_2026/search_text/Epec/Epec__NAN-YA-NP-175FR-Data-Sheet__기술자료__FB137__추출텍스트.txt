Recommended press cycles:




Suggestions:
1. Heating rate of material between 70℃ and 140℃
   1-3℃/min is acceptable.
   1.5-2.5℃/min would be better.
2. Temperature of material over 170℃ must be held for at least 60min. to allow epoxy resin to fully cure.
3. The pressure should be kept below 100psi during cooling to ambient temperature.
4. Cooling rate of material should be kept under 2.5℃/min when the temperature of material is over 100℃, in order to avoid introducing
   twist.
