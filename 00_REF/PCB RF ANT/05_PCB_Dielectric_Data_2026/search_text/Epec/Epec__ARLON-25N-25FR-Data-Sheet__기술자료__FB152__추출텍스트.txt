Material Availability:
25N and 25FR materials are available in rigid or thin copper-clad laminates or B-stage
bonding plies (prepregs), making them ideal for single- and double-sided PWBs and com-
plex multilayer circuits, including dual offset strip line circuitry. Laminates are supplied with
1/2, 1, or 2 ounce H.T.E. electrodeposited copper on both sides. Contact customer service
about other laminate options.
