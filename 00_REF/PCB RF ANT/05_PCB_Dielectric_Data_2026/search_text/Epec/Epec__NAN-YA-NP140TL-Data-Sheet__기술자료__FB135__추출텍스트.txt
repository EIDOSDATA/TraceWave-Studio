Suggestions:
1. Heating rate of material between 70к (158л) and 140к (284л)
   1-3к/min (1.8-5.4л/min) is acceptable.
   1.5-2.5к/min (2.7-4.5л/min) would be better.
2. Temperature of material over 170к/min(ˆˆˋл) must be held for at least 40min. to allow epoxy resin to fully cure.
3. The pressure should be kept below 100psi during cooling to amibient temperature.
4. Cooling rate of material should be kept under 2.5к/min(4.5л/min) when the temperature of material is over 100к (212л), in order to
   avoid introducing twist.
