PTFE/Nonwoven Fiberglass Laminates
                                                                             IsoClad laminates are nonwoven fiberglass/PTFE
                                                                             composites for use as printed circuit board
                                                                             substrates. The nonwoven reinforcement allows
 Features:                                                                   these laminates to be used more easily in
  • Nonwoven Fiberglass Reinforcement                                        applications where the final circuit will be bent to
  • Low Dielectric Constant                                                  shape. Conformal or “wrap-around” antennas are
  • Extremely Low Loss                                                       a good example.

 Benefits:                                                                   IsoClad products use longer random fibers and a
  • Less Rigid than Woven Fiberglass                                         proprietary process to provide greater dimensional
  • Highly Isotropic in X, Y and Z Directions                                stability and better dielectric constant uniformity
                                                                             than competitive nonwoven fiberglass/PTFE
 Typical Applications:                                                       laminates of similar dielectric constants.
  • Conformal Antennas
  • Stripline and Microstrip Circuits                                        IsoClad 917 laminates (Er=2.17, 2.20) use a low
  • Missile Guidance Systems                                                 ratio of fiberglass/PTFE to achieve the lowest
  • Radar and Electronic Warfare Systems                                     dielectric constant and dissipation factor available
                                                                             in a combination of PTFE and fiberglass.

                                                                             IsoClad 933 laminates (Er=2.33) use a higher
                                                                             fiberglass/PTFE ratio for a more highly reinforced
                                                                             combination that offers better dimensional
                                                                             stability and increased mechanical strength.
