Notably, the standard deviation of the PIM values using the IM1/IM1 cladding
was found to be significantly improved vs the standard S1/S1 reverse treated

affords the antenna designer the opportunity to either increase the average PIM
performance over current designs or maintain the current PIM levels while improving
antenna production yields.
