Ultra Low Loss No Flow & Low Flow Prepreg



General Information
VT-462SH (Ultra low loss) No Flow and Low Flow product is designed to meet friendly environment requirement. It has good
bonding and thermal performance in applications of heat sink bonding and rigid-flex board, and have a minimal flow range
with a consistent lamination.
> High Speed / Ultra Low Loss
> Halogen Free & Lead Free Compatible
> Optimized flow range
