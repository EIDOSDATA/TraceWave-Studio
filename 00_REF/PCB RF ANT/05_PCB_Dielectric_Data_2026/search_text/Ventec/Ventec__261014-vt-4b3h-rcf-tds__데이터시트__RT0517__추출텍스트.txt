Thermally Conductive Resin Coated Film Bondply


Resin Coated Film (RCF) Bondply is an unreinforced adhesive system coated onto PET film for use in
high performance and high reliability multilayer PCB stack-ups.
VT-4B3H is a high Tg, ceramic-filled, thermally conductive (3.2 W/mK), halogen-free thermoset resin system,
specifically designed for use in multilayer PCBs requiring enhanced levels of heat management and thermal
conductivity. Thermal-bond RCF can be combined with other Ventec laminate & prepreg systems including
tec-thermal IMS, VT-5A2 and VT-4A2H thermally conductive laminates & prepregs in hybrid stackups.
