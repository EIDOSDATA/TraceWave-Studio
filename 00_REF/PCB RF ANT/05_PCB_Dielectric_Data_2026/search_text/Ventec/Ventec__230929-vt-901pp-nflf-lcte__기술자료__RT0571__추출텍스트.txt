Polyimide No Flow / Low Flow Prepreg



General Information
Polyimide Low and No Flow product using novel chemistry for flow control and enhanced bond strength designed for use in polyimide
rigid-flex applications.


> Improved for high layer count rigid-flex
> Tg 200˚C
> Low Z-CTE
> Meets IPC-4101E Specification sheets /40, /41 & /42
> Compatible with Lead Free processing
