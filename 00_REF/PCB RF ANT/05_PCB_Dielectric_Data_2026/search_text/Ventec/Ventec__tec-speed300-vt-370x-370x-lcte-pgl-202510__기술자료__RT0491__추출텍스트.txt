•       Laminates should be stored flat in a clean dry area. Cores will benefit from being stored between two
        stiffeners in order to prevent unnecessary bending of the layers or damaged corners. Soft slip sheets should
        be used to prevent dust and debris from be embossed into the material.
•       Laminates are without woven glass reinforcement and are softer than most other rigid base materials for
        printed circuit board and are more susceptible to handling damage. As with all laminate materials, especially
        thin materials, care should be taken to avoid distorting the material during handling.



Designing and Inner layer Process
•       Materials are compatible with many tooling systems. Choosing whether to use round or slotted pins, external
        or internal pinning. Whichever approach is used, it is good practice to retain copper around tooling holes.
•       A chemical process consisting of organic cleaners and a micro-etch is the preferred method of preparing
        copper surfaces for coating with liquid or film photoresist. Mechanical scrubbing is not recommended.
•       Developers, strippers, and copper etchants used to process epoxy glass materials will also work with layers.
        Thin cores may require leader boards for conveyorized processing and frames or supportive racks for
        vertical-type processing.
•       Laminates are compatible with most oxide and oxide alternative processes. Highly caustic, high temperature
        processes, should be followed by a thorough rinse and bake of the inner layers.

Bonding for Multilayer
•             Special pretreatments of etched surfaces using plasma processes shouldn’t be necessary providing care was
              taken to protect the substrate surface after copper etch. Inner-layers should be baked at 120℃ for 60
              minutes to ensure removal of volatile substances prior to MLB bonding.
•             Cores are compatible with a broad range of thermosetting adhesive systems. Many factors, such as electrical
              performance, flow characteristics, ease of processing, and bond temperature requirements are considered
              when making the best overall choice.
•             The press cycle is determined by the requirements of the chosen adhesive resin system of High Tg prepreg.



Bond cycle
1.Heating rate (Rate of Rise) of material [Material Temperature]:

50-315 ºC: ≥5.5℃/min 315-371 ºC≥2.7℃/min
2.Curing Temperature & Time: 30-60min at more than 371ºC.
3.Full Pressure: 250-500psi should be applied full pressure before 23 ºC.
4.Cooling rate of material: 260-121 ºC ≤1.1℃/min.
5.Cushion for pressure evenness is needed.

PROCESS GUIDE


Plating and EDS
•       Laminates have been engineered to be highly resistant to chemical infiltration and a robust hole wall  

        preparation process is necessary for a successful deposition plating process. Following hole wall
        preparation, they will accept standard electroless copper or direct deposit metallization plating process.
•       Image, Develop, Etch, Strip Film.

        When copper surface preparation is required, chemical cleaning processes are preferred. Mechanical
        scrubbing should be avoided due to possible mechanical damage. Otherwise, standard processing should be
        used.



Solder mask and finish
•       Materials should be rinsed and baked prior to solder mask application. Rinsing in warm or hot water for
        20-30 minutes followed by 60 minutes @125℃should be sufficient, especially if the bake is done under
        vacuum.
•       No special treatment is required if the surface has not been mechanically scrubbed. In rare cases, where  

        adhesion is poor, a plasma treatment may be used to activate the exposed PTFE.
•       Laminate is compatible to most final finishes (HASL, Sn, Ag, Ni/Au, OSP, etc). A rinse/bake should be done

        prior to HASL. Make sure etched substrate surfaces are clean before ENIG plating, or else background
        plating could occur.

Packaging and baking recommendation
•       It is recommended to bake the board before packaging at 125℃/4~8h to avoid moisture causing a decrease
        in heat resistance.
•       If the PCBs needs to be stored for a long time before use, it is recommended to use aluminum foil vacuum
        packaging.
•       If exceed 3 months after packaging, It is best to bake the PCBs at 125℃/4~6h before assembly before use.
