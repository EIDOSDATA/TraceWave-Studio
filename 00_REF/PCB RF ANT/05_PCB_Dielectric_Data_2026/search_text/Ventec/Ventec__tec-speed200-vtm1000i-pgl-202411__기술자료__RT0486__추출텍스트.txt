Designing and Inner layer Process
•       Tooling & pinning holes should be drilled or routed. Punching is forbidden.
•       Dimension stability is superior to Standard FR-4 materials.
•       Please consult with your oxide vendor to make sure that our material is fully compatible with your oxide
        process. We recommend controlling the peel strength to over 2 lb./in.
•       After oxide, it is preferred to press cores within 12 hours. Any cores staged more than 24 hours should be
        re-treated.
•       Please confirm the grinding treatment process as the material was soft and brittle. Please don't use the
        grinding process for the inner and outer layer with large area or full no copper design.

Desmear Process
Panels should be baked for at least 1 hour at 120℃ prior to plasma treatment. Plasma treated holes are more delicate
than permanganate etched holes. Panels should not be exposed to any pressure wash or scrubbing process prior to
metallization.

Plasma Desmear: 

One time of FR-4 conditions is recommendable.

Permanganate horizontal Desmear: 

The weight loss of VTM1000i laminate is more than that of our conventional FR-4 material. One time of FR-4
condition is recommendable.

Permanganate vertical Desmear: 

Vertical desmear process is not recommendable.

Packaging and baking recommendation
•              It is recommended to bake the board before packaging at 125℃/4~8h to avoid moisture causing a decrease
               in heat resistance.
•              If the PCBs needs to be stored for a long time before use, it is recommended to use aluminum foil 

               vacuum packaging.
•              If exceed 3 months after packaging, It is best to bake the PCBs at 125℃/4~6h before assembly before use.
