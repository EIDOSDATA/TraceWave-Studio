No Flow & Low Flow Prepreg



General Information
VT-447 No Flow and Low Flow product is designed to meet friendly environment requirement. It has good bonding and thermal
performance in applications of heat sink bonding and rigid-flex board, and have a minimal flow range with a consistent lamination.


> High Tg & High Td
> Halogen Free & Lead Free Compatible
> IPC-4101E /127 /128 /130
> Optimized flow range for camera module
