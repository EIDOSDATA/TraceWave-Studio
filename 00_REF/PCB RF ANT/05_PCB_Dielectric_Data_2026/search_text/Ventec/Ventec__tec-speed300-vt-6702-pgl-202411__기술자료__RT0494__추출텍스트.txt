•       Take care in handling thin core laminates as they are easily damaged.
•       The grain direction is indicated on the label with an arrow.
•       Laminates exceeding 24 months can still be used as long as the copper foil has no severe oxidation or stain.




Inner Layer Process
•       Tooling & pinning holes should be drilled or routed. Punching is forbidden.
•       Lay the panel on a flat clean surface. The solvent clean should be followed with a clean water rinse.
•       Remove copper oxides using commercially available cleaners and micro etchants.
•       Abrasive cleaning (e.g., pumice powder or conveyorized brush scrubbing) is not recommended.

•       Mechanical surface preparation tends to cause microscopic scratches in copper. These scratches become
        stress risers that can lead to thermal stress cracks in some environmental conditions. While strongly
        recommending against abrasive cleaning, we recognize that in some circumstances that abrasive cleaning
        is necessary. When abrasive cleaning is required, it should be performed with minimal pressure and
        knowledge of the risks involved. Brush scrubbing may cause material distortion.
•       After oxide, it is preferred to press cores within 12 hours. Any cores staged more than 24 hours should be
        re-treated.

Desmear Process
Plasma treatment is necessary process, and material should be baked more than 1 hour at 120℃ before plasma
treatment. Meanwhile, material cannot be suffered any high pressure washing and scrubbing before metallization.

Plasma: Half time of FR-4 conditions is recommended.

Plating and solder mask
•       VT-6702 laminates have been engineered to be highly resistant to chemical infiltration and a robust hole wall  

        preparation process is necessary for a successful deposition plating process. Following hole wall preparation,
        VT-6702 will accept standard electroless copper or direct deposit metallization plating process.
•       When copper surface preparation is required, chemical cleaning processes are preferred.Mechanical  

        scrubbing should be avoided due to possible mechanical damage. Otherwise, standard processing should be
        used.
•       No special treatment is required if the surface has not been mechanically scrubbed. In rare cases, where

        adhesion is poor, a plasma treatment may be used to activate the exposed PTFE.

Packaging and baking recommendation
•       It is recommended to bake the board before packaging at 125℃/4~8h to avoid moisture causing a decrease
        in heat resistance.
•       If the PCBs needs to be stored for a long time before use, it is recommended to use aluminum foil vacuum
        packaging.
•       If exceed 3 months after packaging, It is best to bake the PCBs at 125℃/4~6h before assembly before use.
