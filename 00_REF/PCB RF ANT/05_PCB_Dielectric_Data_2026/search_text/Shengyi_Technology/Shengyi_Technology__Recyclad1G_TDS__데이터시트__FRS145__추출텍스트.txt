STORAGE CONDITION


   3 months when stored at < 23℃ and <50% RH.

   6 months when stored at <5℃. Normalize in room temperature for at least 4h before using.

   Beware of moisture, always keep wrapped in damp-proof material. Keeping in normal condition,

    prepreg might absorb moisture and its bonding strength would be weakened.
